How the metrics are defined

Category / sub-category
Split of the ticket's latest title ("Category|Sub-category"). Case differences are merged.
Queue
CURRENT_QUEUE on the ticket (Px, Cx, Booking Status, Wiom Net, Tech, …).
Resolved ≤ Xh
Share of all tickets created in the period whose final resolution came within X calendar hours of creation (RESOLUTION_PERIOD_MINS_CALENDARHRS). Open tickets count as not resolved, so the latest days read slightly low until they age.
> 72h
Resolved, but after more than 72 hours. Open = not resolved yet.
Reopen rate
Tickets reopened at least once (TIMES_REOPENED > 0) ÷ resolved tickets.
Repeat in 7 days
Ticket from a customer who had raised another ticket in the previous 7 days.
Per day / Δ
Partial periods (Oct MTD = 20 days, Oct W3 = 6 days) are compared per day. Δ is against the previous period; pp = percentage points.

Ticket volume comparison

Tickets created per period. Δ compares tickets per day in the latest period with the one before it.

Resolution within time limits

Cumulative share of tickets resolved within 4, 12, 24, 48 and 72 hours of creation. Δ ≤24h is against the previous period.

Resolved ≤24h across periods

Same breakdown as above, ≤24h only, so the trend for each row reads across.

Reopen rate comparison

Tickets reopened at least once ÷ resolved tickets. Δ is in percentage points against the previous period.

Open tickets by age

Tickets created since Jul 1 that are still unresolved, by hours since creation.
