How the metrics are defined

Category / sub-category
Split of the ticket's latest title ("Category|Sub-category"). Case differences are merged.
Workstream
CX L2 = queue Cx. PFT = Internet Issues, Partner Misbehavior, Shifting Request (Shift Within My Home, Shift to New Address) and Others (except Other, App not working, Invoice requested), not in queue Cx. Tickets in neither (e.g. Disconnect, Payment Issues, Refund) are not a workstream: they appear under "All workstreams" and in every other cut, but not as a workstream of their own.
Chat / call
Chat = TICKET_SOURCE CUSTOMER_CHAT. Call = every other ticket: IVR and Kapture sources until Jul 17; since Jul 18 call tickets arrive with the source left blank.
Queue
CURRENT_QUEUE on the ticket (Px, Cx, Booking Status, Wiom Net, Tech, …).
Resolved ≤ Xh
Share of all tickets created in the period whose final resolution came within X calendar hours of creation (RESOLUTION_PERIOD_MINS_CALENDARHRS). Open tickets count as not resolved, so the latest days read slightly low until they age.
> 72h
Resolved, but after more than 72 hours. Open = not resolved yet.
P50 / P90 / P95
Resolution time (calendar hours) that 50% / 90% / 95% of resolved tickets fell within. Worked out from a resolution-time histogram so it follows the filters: exact to within 15 minutes below 24h, 1 hour up to 7 days, 1 day beyond.
Reopen rate
Tickets reopened at least once (TIMES_REOPENED > 0) ÷ resolved tickets.
Repeat in 7 days
Ticket from a customer who had raised another ticket in the previous 7 days.
Created between
Pick a From and To date (created date, IST). Every table then gets a Selected dates view, compared with the same number of days just before; the overview cards switch to the selected dates too.
Per day / Δ
Partial periods (the current month so far and the current week) are compared per day. In "This month by day" each column is one created date and Δ compares it with the day before; today's column is a partial day. Δ is against the previous period; pp = percentage points.

Ticket volume comparison

Tickets created per period. Δ compares tickets per day in the latest period with the one before it.

Resolution within time limits

Cumulative share of tickets resolved within 4, 12, 24, 48 and 72 hours of creation. Δ ≤24h is against the previous period.

Resolved ≤24h across periods

Same breakdown as above, ≤24h only, so the trend for each row reads across.

Reopen rate comparison

Tickets reopened at least once ÷ resolved tickets. Δ is in percentage points against the previous period.

P95 resolution time comparison

Hours from ticket creation to final resolution within which 95% of resolved tickets were closed. Lower is better. Δ is in hours against the previous period.

Open tickets by age

Tickets created in the dashboard period that are still unresolved, by hours since creation.

Zone scorecard

Per zone for the chosen month. Δ per day is against the month before.

Partner watchlist, this month
